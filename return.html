Return & Exchange 🔄

Please read our policy before placing your order.

No Return Policy

We do not accept returns for products that are received in good and undamaged condition.

Please check your product carefully after receiving your order.

Damaged Product

If you receive a damaged product, you may be eligible for an exchange.

Exchange is available only for products that are received damaged.

Photo & Video Proof

To request an exchange for a damaged product, you must share clear photos and a proper unboxing video showing the damage.

The provided photos and video will be reviewed before an exchange is approved.

Report Within 2 Days

Any damaged product issue must be reported within 2 days of delivery.

Requests received after this time period may not be accepted.

No Refunds

We do not provide refunds for returned or damaged products.

If the damage is verified and the request is approved, only an exchange will be provided.

Please record an unboxing video while opening your parcel. This may be required as proof if your product arrives damaged.
