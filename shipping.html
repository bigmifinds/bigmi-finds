Shipping Policy 🚚

Everything you need to know about shipping and delivery.

Shipping Charges

The minimum order value is ₹199.

Orders of ₹499 or more are eligible for FREE SHIPPING.

For orders between ₹199 and ₹499, shipping charges will be calculated according to the product and applicable shipping cost.

How Shipping Charges Work

Shipping charges are based on the products added to your order.

The applicable shipping charge will be automatically calculated and shown in your cart before checkout.

Once your cart total reaches ₹499 or more, the shipping charge will become FREE.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available only for customers in Berhampur.

COD availability may be confirmed based on the delivery location entered during checkout.

Prepaid Orders

Prepaid payment is available for customers across India.

Customers outside Berhampur can place their orders using the available prepaid payment option.

Delivery Time

Orders are expected to be delivered within 7 days.

Delivery time may vary depending on the delivery location and courier service.
